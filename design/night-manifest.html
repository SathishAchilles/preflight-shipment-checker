§ 01 — Rulebook

§ 02 — Process

Three steps to cleared.

No login, no server. It runs entirely in your browser.

Step I

Describe it

Type the shipment in plain words. Preflight fills in the form and asks only for what's missing. Dates are read day-first.

Step II

Inspect it

All ten rules run against the carrier your priority selects, along with data-quality checks on every cell.

Step III

Correct it

Fix with one click. Every change re-runs the checks and records what changed, e.g. "R-05 now passes".
